A card purchase on the 5th that you pay off on the 28th lands on the 28th — because that's when the money actually goes.

Cards and savings are outflows, not ledgers.

Card payment — one line on the timeline

Savings transfer — one line on the timeline

Nothing to reconcile, nothing to keep in sync. One account, one timeline.

Income belongs on the timeline too

Money you're owed is
part of the forecast.

A bill you forget to tick

makes the forecast pessimistic —
you have more than it says.

A payment that never arrives

does the opposite — the forecast
promises money you don't have.

So income is watched the same way bills are. And anything paid by someone else stays in your history but out of the forecast — the number you see is the number you have.

On your Home Screen

The calendar comes to
the Home Screen.

Four widgets — today's plan, your balance, the weeks around today, and the whole month. Each has its own settings, and tapping a day opens the app right there.

One rule we kept on purpose: while you test a purchase in “What if?”, widgets don't move. The Home Screen only ever shows what's real — and a widget reads a snapshot on your device, never the network.
