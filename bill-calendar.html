Bill calendar

Bills on their dates, and what is left after each

A list of bills says what you owe. A bill calendar says when — and what your balance will be once each one has gone out.

Download on the App Store

Each bill on the day it is due

Add a bill with its amount, its date and how often it repeats — weekly, every two weeks, monthly — or once. The calendar places every one ahead, and each day shows the balance it ends on, so a bill's effect shows on its own date, not at the end of the month.

What is left after each

Tap a day to see everything on it, with the balance that day ends on beside the date. Scroll, and the days after it follow, each with its own bills.

Paid, and still to pay

When a bill has gone out, swipe its row right to mark it done. Bills from past days that you never marked show on the balance card as Overdue, with their total, and the card changes colour: the balance on it may not be true yet.

Private by default

Local by default, and no account to start. Sync, when you turn it on, is end-to-end encrypted with a passphrase we cannot read or recover. No bank login — the app never asks for a bank's credentials.
