What a money calendar shows, one question at a time

Each guide is one screen of JayCK Flow and the question it answers.

  • Future balanceWhat a future balance is, and how a calendar shows itYour future balance is what your account will hold on a given day, once the paydays and bills before it have landed. JayCK Flow shows it under every date.
  • Cash flow calendarIncome and bills on their dates, the balance in betweenA cash flow calendar puts every paycheck and every bill on the day it lands and shows the balance on each day in between. JayCK Flow, for iPhone and iPad.
  • Bill calendarBills on their dates, and what is left after eachPut each bill on the day it is due, with how often it repeats, and see the balance left after it. JayCK Flow is a money calendar for iPhone and iPad.

Every screen shown is the app itself, with demo figures.
